## pansy 0.1.7

### Your agents from your phone

- Settings › Network and sharing › Phone and other devices pairs a phone with a QR code. On the phone's browser, start and continue Codex and Claude Code threads at home: approve, answer their questions, steer, queue and stop, see each turn's changes, commit, push and open a pull request.
- Speak instead of typing: a microphone in the composer writes what you say where the cursor is (iOS and macOS, and browsers on HTTPS).
- While a thread runs for your phone, this computer doesn't go to sleep when idle. Closing the lid still sleeps it.
- Installed as a service, pansy keeps your phone's threads going with the window closed.

### Agents and providers

- T3 Code runs on Codex too, beside Claude Code, through pansy's gateway.
- Gemini Code Assist says what it risks before you sign in: Google may suspend an account used outside Gemini CLI.
- Antigravity Cloak is off the curated list.

### The window

- T3 Code's type scale: whole pixel sizes and lighter weights, and thinner text on macOS.
- Arch Linux and Omarchy: `yay -S pansy-bin`.
